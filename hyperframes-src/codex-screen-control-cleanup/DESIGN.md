# Design

Concept angle: a Canary-yellow safety scanner makes cautious process cleanup
visible as a precise mechanical sequence, not an alarm.

- Canvas: deep blue-black `#071017` with localized steel-blue radial light.
- Brand accent: Canary yellow `#FFC928`; verified state `#4BD38A`; warning `#FF6F66`.
- Display: Archivo Black. Operational copy and data: JetBrains Mono.
- Focal element: the scan beam and the process identity card.
- Edge anchors: Canaryd mark at top-left; policy metadata at bottom edge.
- Supporting detail: bounded process rows, observation counter, exact identity seal.
- Background: fixed grid and localized radial glows; no full-screen linear gradients.
- Motion: mechanical scans, status locks, and one controlled collapse on SIGTERM.
